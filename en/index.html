A CLEAR PATH TO YOUR FILES

FIND YOUR WAY

Follow the folders.
Find your destination.

01

Navigate with context

A folder tree and current path help you find parent folders and destinations. Search managed folders by file or folder name.

02

Copy, move, and paste

Select files to copy or move, then paste them at your destination. Rename, share, and preview files along the way.

03

Save from other apps

Use the share sheet in Photos or Safari to save directly into a managed folder in Pathly.

04

Connect to Drive and SMB

Browse Google Drive, create and rename folders, move items to trash, and preview and share files. Access files on SMB shares, too.

YOUR FILES, YOUR CHOICE

You choose
what to connect.

Google Drive connects when you sign in and grant permission. Drive files and metadata are used to display your files and carry out the file operations you request.

Your iPhone communicates directly with Google. Authentication information is stored in the device's Keychain. Pathly does not send this data to a server operated by Laconix. When you share or export a file, it is passed to the app or destination you choose.

You can remove saved sign-in information in Pathly and revoke access through your Google Account. Google user data is not used for advertising or to train general-purpose AI models.
