Pathly Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

About Pathly · 日本語

Laconix describes below how information is handled in Pathly for iOS. This policy applies to the initial release focused on managed folders on the device.

1. Information and purposes

Pathly handles files, folders, names, paths and modification dates that you select to provide browsing, search, copy, move, preview, sharing and export. Managed folder bookmarks, display settings, history and app lock settings are stored on your device.

2. Storage and transmission

Information is stored on your device, in the iOS App Group container, or at a destination you choose. Laconix operates no server collecting your in-app files or settings. Folders selected using Files may be handled by a file provider under that provider's storage, network and privacy policies.

If you request a download by sharing a URL, Pathly connects to the website you specify. Sharing and export pass selected files to the app or destination you choose.

3. Purchases and authentication

Apple StoreKit provides product identifiers, purchase results and entitlement status. We do not receive credit card details. App lock uses only the iOS authentication result; the app does not collect or store face or fingerprint data. App lock controls access to the Pathly interface. It does not encrypt files or restrict access through Files or other apps.

4. Third-party services

iOS, StoreKit, the App Store, and the file providers, sharing destinations and download sources you choose process information under their respective policies. See the Apple Privacy Policy. Pathly includes no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.

5. Disclosure

We do not sell your information or disclose it for advertising. Disclosure is limited to operations you request, legal requirements, and necessary protection of rights and safety.

6. Retention and deletion

Settings and history remain on the device until removed in the app or by deleting the app. Files created under On My iPhone > Pathly are removed when the app is deleted. Manage exported files and files stored elsewhere at their destination. The app or iOS may delete temporary files.

7. Security

Pathly uses the iOS sandbox, App Groups and system authentication. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed completely secure. Back up important files.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as features or legal requirements change. Important changes will be announced here or in the app. Updates apply when published.
